# Slop banlist

`slop-killer.ts` scans every draft for these. Fatal phrases under "Forbidden phrases" fail; other slop-pattern hits warn for writer judgement. Each entry has a category for the linter's report. The user's edits in `/review-tray` and the Sheet's `Edits` column feed back to grow this list automatically.

## Latinate filler (kill on sight)

- leverage (verb)
- delve
- navigate (the landscape | the complexities)
- realm
- myriad
- tapestry
- intricate
- ostensibly
- albeit (overused)
- whilst (use "while")

## Hype words

- cutting-edge
- world-class
- best-in-class
- game-changer
- next-level
- unparalleled
- transformative (allowed only for actual transformation programs)
- pioneering
- groundbreaking
- robust (unless paired with a concrete measurement)
- seamless
- holistic (unless in a UX-design context)
- bespoke (unless describing actual custom build)

## Vague action verbs (must be paired with a concrete outcome)

- orchestrated
- spearheaded
- championed
- pioneered
- drove (unless paired with measurable result)
- led (always pair with team size + outcome)
- managed (always pair with scope + outcome)

## Connective scaffolding

- moreover
- furthermore
- additionally (use "also")
- consequently
- in conclusion
- it's important to note
- it's worth noting
- in today's fast-paced world
- in today's digital age

## Hollow openers (cover-letter / DM / email)

- "I am writing to…"
- "I hope this message finds you well"
- "I'm thrilled to…"
- "I'm excited to apply for…"
- "I'm reaching out to…"
- "As a [job title] with X years of experience…"
- "I am a results-driven…"
- "I am passionate about…"

## Empty closers

- "Please do not hesitate to reach out"
- "Thank you for your time and consideration"
- "I look forward to the opportunity to discuss"
- "Eager to hear back"

## Generic praise

- "I was particularly impressed by your company's mission…"
- "Your company stands out for…"
- "I admire your commitment to innovation"

## Construction tics

- "Not just X, but Y"
- "Tricolons of abstract nouns" — flag any three-comma list of abstractions (passion, integrity, innovation)
- "Harness/unlock/drive" + abstract noun: "harness the power of", "unlock potential", "drive value", "bridge the gap"
- "X is not Y — it's Z" (clever-clever rhetorical)

## Hedging stacks

- "It might be worth considering whether perhaps"
- "I would love to potentially"
- "could possibly"

## Industry-specific overlay (IT / consulting / AU government)

- "synergy", "synergies"
- "stakeholder alignment" (overused — replace with the specific stakeholder + the specific alignment)
- "operational excellence" (unless paired with a measurable lift)
- "future-proof"
- "digital-first" / "mobile-first" (often vacuous filler)

## Punctuation tics

- Triple ellipses (…) anywhere they shouldn't be
- Smart-quote / straight-quote mixing
- Double em-dash sentences (— sentence has two em-dashes —)
- Bullet glyphs other than the project's chosen bullet style

## Forbidden phrases (instant fail, never)

- "As an AI language model"
- "I'd be more than happy to"
- "Feel free to reach out at your convenience"
